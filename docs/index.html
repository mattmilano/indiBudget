User-Defined Rules

Batch Categorization

Select multiple uncategorized transactions and apply a category to all of them at once. The keyword pattern is automatically saved as a rule for future imports.

Rules Management

View, edit, and delete your custom categorization rules. Your rules take priority over built-in patterns for personalized categorization.

Priority System

User-defined rules have the highest priority (100), followed by specific patterns (10), then generic patterns (0). This ensures your preferences always win.

Predictive Categorization

When detecting recurring payments, the system suggests categories based on your rules, helping you predict and label upcoming bills.
